# Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Content Engine is a personal tool used only by its owner with her own Google account.

**What it accesses.** youtube.upload: to upload the owner's own videos to her own channel as private videos. youtube.readonly: to check the processing status of those uploads.

**What it stores.** The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about anyone else is collected.

Content Engine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

**Revoking access:** https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
